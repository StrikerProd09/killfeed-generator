How to Use

In the page, you can customize Player 1 and Player 2 names, enable the headshot icon, download a single item, or download all items at once.

To download a single item:

  1. Left-click on the item you want to download.
  2. Scroll to the bottom of the page and click the Convert button to transform the HTML element into a canvas.
  3. use the Download button or Right-click on the generated canvas element, select Save image as..., and choose a name and location for your file.
