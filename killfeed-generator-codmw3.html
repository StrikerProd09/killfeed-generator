Guide

How to use

From picking a killfeed to downloading the final image.

Pick a weapon, open the Customizer to set player names, colors and item style, then download a high-resolution image from the Export section.

To download a single item:

  1. Pick the weapon you want to download.
  2. Use the Customizer to set names, colors and item style. The preview updates as you adjust settings.
  3. In the Export section, press Download image. The image refreshes automatically, so it always matches the preview.
